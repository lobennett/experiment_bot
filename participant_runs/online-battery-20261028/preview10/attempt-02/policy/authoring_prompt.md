Write a participant policy for the observed task. Follow its instructions as a typical attentive adult participant. Decide all responses and their timing from the current participant-visible observation and the policy's own history. Use a seeded local random generator if the policy makes random choices.

Observed instructions: identify the ink color of the displayed word, ignoring word identity. Comma responds to red ink, period to blue ink, slash to green ink. Practice supplies feedback and color reminders; test removes them. Respond as quickly and accurately as possible.

Observation interface: an object contains a currently rendered stimulus or null. A stimulus contains displayed text, computed visible color, geometry, and an observation-side identity for the current DOM presentation. Visible absence or replacement establishes a new opportunity. The policy receives no task state, correctness, future trial, or platform data. Input interface accepts an ordinary keyboard key. Return a key decision or null to wait. A response is sent once per visible presentation, with current visible state checked again before delivery. The adapter polls and yields at instruction/block boundaries; these are operational limits, not participant timing targets.
